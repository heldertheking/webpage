## Overview

This site (heldertheking.com) is a personal portfolio. It does not run ads, does not sell data, and does not use any tracking cookies. This page explains exactly what data is collected when you visit, and what happens to anything you submit through the contact form.

## Cookies

This site does not set any cookies - no analytics cookies, no advertising cookies, no session cookies. There is no cookie consent banner because there is nothing to consent to.

Your light/dark theme preference is saved in your browser's `localStorage` (not a cookie) under the key `theme`. It never leaves your browser, isn't sent to any server, and is only used to remember which theme to show you next time.

## Analytics - Cloudflare Web Analytics

This site uses [Cloudflare Web Analytics](https://www.cloudflare.com/web-analytics/) to see aggregate traffic (roughly how many people visit, which pages, and how fast pages load). It's designed to be privacy-first:

- It does not use cookies or any client-side persistent identifier.
- It does not fingerprint your device or track you across other websites.
- It does not collect personal data such as your name, email, or exact IP address for tracking purposes.
- Data is reported in aggregate - Cloudflare's beacon script loads from `static.cloudflareinsights.com` and reports page views to `cloudflareinsights.com`.

See Cloudflare's own [Web Analytics data & metrics documentation](https://developers.cloudflare.com/web-analytics/data-metrics/) for the full technical detail on exactly what it measures.

## Contact form

If you use the contact form, the email address and message you type - along with your current light/dark theme and a timestamp - are sent directly to my own self-hosted automation server (automation.heldertheking.com) so I can read and reply to you. Nothing is submitted unless you press "Send message," and this data is only used to respond to your message - it is never sold, shared with third parties, or used for marketing.

## Other requests made by this site

The homepage also fetches a small status badge (uptime/build status) from automation.heldertheking.com. That request doesn't send any data about you - it just asks my own server for its current status to display on the page.

## Questions

If you have any questions about data handling on this site, reach out through the [contact form](/#contact) on the homepage.
