## 3. Web Hosting & Server Log Files

This website is hosted using Cloudflare. When you visit this site, standard technical information (server log files) such as your browser type, operating system, referrer URL, host name of the accessing computer, and time of the server request are processed by the hosting infrastructure to ensure site security and stable operation.

## 4. Local Storage (Theme Preference)

This site does not set tracking or session cookies.

Your light/dark theme preference is stored locally on your device using your browser's `localStorage` under the key `theme`. This data remains entirely inside your browser, is never sent to any server, and is used solely to remember your display preference between visits.

## 5. Analytics (Cloudflare Web Analytics)

This site uses **Cloudflare Web Analytics** (provided by Cloudflare, Inc.) to measure aggregate traffic (such as page view counts, top pages, and loading speed).

* It does not use cookies or persistent client identifiers.
* It does not perform device fingerprinting or cross-site tracking.
* It does not collect personal data (such as your name, email address, or full IP address for individual tracking).
* All analytics data is processed in aggregate. Technical beacon requests are sent from `static.cloudflareinsights.com` to `cloudflareinsights.com`.

For further details, refer to [Cloudflare's Web Analytics Documentation](https://developers.cloudflare.com/web-analytics/data-metrics/).

## 6. Contact Form & Data Processing

If you submit an inquiry through the contact form, the data you enter (email address, message, light/dark theme state, and timestamp) is transmitted to a self-hosted automation server (`automation.heldertheking.com`).

* **Purpose:** The processing of this data is strictly limited to reviewing and responding to your inquiry.
* **Retention:** Submitted messages are retained only as long as necessary to address your request or follow up on communications.
* **Sharing:** Your data is never sold, shared with unauthorized third parties, or used for marketing purposes.

## 7. Status Requests

The homepage retrieves a status badge (uptime/build status) directly from `automation.heldertheking.com`. This request only checks server status to render the badge and does not transmit personal user data.

## 8. Your Rights

Under the Swiss Data Protection Act, you have the right to:

* Request information regarding personal data held about you.
* Request the correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal data.
* Request the deletion of your personal data, subject to statutory retention obligations.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact the controller via the email listed above or through the site's contact form.
